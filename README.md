# Regional-Sales-Analysis
Regional Sales Analysis is the process of examining and comparing sales performance across different geographical regions. It helps organizations understand which areas are performing well, which need improvement, and what factors influence regional differences in sales.

The analysis provides insights into market demand, customer preferences, revenue contribution, and operational performance across regions such as North, South, East, and West (or states/cities).



⭐ Objectives of Regional Sales Analysis

Compare sales performance across regions.

Identify high-performing and low-performing regions.

Analyze revenue contribution and sales trends by location.

Understand customer behavior and product demand by region.

Support decision-making in marketing, supply chain, and inventory management.


🟩 Insights You Can Derive

A Regional Sales Analysis helps answer:

Which region contributes the most revenue?

Which region has the fastest-growing sales trend?

Which region needs more marketing campaigns?

Which region has low stock movement or low customer activity?

What products perform best in which regions?
